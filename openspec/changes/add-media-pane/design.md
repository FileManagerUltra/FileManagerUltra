## Context

Preview plays one silent video through an `ffmpeg` child piping RGBA frames (`crates/preview/src/player.rs`) and stops when the selection changes. Panes are views (`PaneKind` in `fm-layout`); behaviour lives in engine commands; the UI thread never blocks; backends are pure Rust with no FFI and no `unsafe`; decoders already run out of process so a crash cannot take the app down.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- Audio and video playback with sound in a dedicated pane, with a queue, playlists and fullscreen.
- Playback independent of selection and pane focus; one place that drives the audio device.
- Everything is a command; the pane is a thin view over session state.
- Same behaviour on Linux, Windows and macOS; a missing decoder or audio device degrades with a hint.

**Non-Goals:**
- Streaming URLs, DRM, DVD/Blu-ray menus, casting, a music library database or tag editing, crossfade, parametric EQ, video filters.
- A queue, playlists or fullscreen in Preview (those live in the Media pane); changing Preview's poster and metadata cards.

## Decisions

1. **New `PaneKind::Media`, queue in `Session`.** The pane renders `Session::media` state; closing or switching the pane does not clear the queue. Audio may continue with the pane closed ("keep playing", default on) and shows a status-bar indicator; video stops with the pane. Alternative (extend Preview) rejected: its lifetime is tied to selection.
2. **New crate `fm-media` at L1** (no egui, no I/O beyond decoder subprocesses and the audio device): `Queue` (items, current, shuffle order, repeat), playlist parse/write (M3U/M3U8/PLS), `AudioSink` trait with a null/fake implementation for tests, `Clock`, decoder control. `engine` owns the single `MediaSession` and ticks it from its job lanes; `ui` only reads snapshots.
3. **Decoding.** Audio-only files `symphonia` can decode (mp3, flac, ogg/vorbis, wav, aac/m4a, alac) play in-process on a dedicated audio thread inside `fm-media`. Everything else, and the soundtrack of videos, uses `ffmpeg` subprocesses discovered like today (`find_tool`): one for RGBA video frames on stdout, one for raw `s16le` PCM. A decoder crash or hang ends playback of that item with an error and advances; it never takes the app down.
4. **Clocking.** The audio sink's played-sample count is the master clock. The video reader keeps only the newest frames; the UI picks the frame whose pts is closest to the clock, dropping late frames and holding early ones. Videos without audio use a monotonic wall clock. Seeking restarts both decoders at the target (with a short cap on seek rate); pause stops the sink and the clock without killing decoders for audio-only playback.
5. **Audio output: `cpal` behind the `AudioSink` trait.** User-approved: ALSA development headers are a build-time dependency on Linux (third exception to "no system libraries", recorded in AGENTS.md and `docs/development.md`; CI installs `libasound2-dev`; `.deb` depends on `libasound2`). PipeWire/PulseAudio users go through their ALSA compatibility layer. Quality comes from the pipeline, not the backend: all decoders output f32 PCM; one `rubato` resample to the device rate; preamp, 10-band biquad EQ, volume and a soft limiter run in f32 on the audio thread. No device means the pane shows a hint and plays silently (video) or errors (audio).
6. **Fullscreen.** Two levels: pane fullscreen (media pane fills the window; other panes hidden, state restored on exit) via `media.fullscreen`, and optional window fullscreen via `window.fullscreen` (`ViewportCommand::Fullscreen`) toggled together. Controls auto-hide after 3 s without pointer movement; Esc, `F`, or double-click leaves. Fullscreen never changes saved normal geometry.
7. **Remote files.** Non-local items are copied through the normal job lane into an owner-only temp spool (same rules as "Open remote file": 0700 folder, size cap, deleted on quit), then played from disk. Progress shows in the pane.
8. **Commands** (all in `engine/src/media_cmds.rs`): `media.play`, `media.pause`, `media.toggle`, `media.stop`, `media.next`, `media.prev`, `media.seek`, `media.volume`, `media.mute`, `media.speed`, `media.enqueue`, `media.play_folder`, `media.remove`, `media.move`, `media.clear`, `media.shuffle`, `media.repeat`, `media.track.audio`, `media.track.subtitle`, `media.step`, `media.fullscreen`, `media.playlist.load`, `media.playlist.save`, plus `window.fullscreen`. Each has id, label, menu path, shortcut, params doc, `enabled` and a bad-params test.
9. **Persistence.** The queue (file paths/URIs only), current index, volume, shuffle and repeat persist in prefs/workspace state; no credentials, owner-only files. Items that no longer exist are skipped with a notice.
10. **Preview keeps simple playback, on the same engine.** Preview's single-file player (video and audio) uses the same `fm-media` decoders, audio sink and clock as the Media pane, but through its own lightweight `PreviewPlayer` owned by the pane (stopped on selection change/close). A session-level `AudioOwner` guarantees one active player: starting either pauses the other. "Play in Media pane" transfers file and position. Audio does not autoplay in Preview by default.
11. **Gapless.** In-process decoding exposes encoder delay/padding (LAME/Xing, iTunSMPB) and pre-opens the next queue item ~5 s before the end, feeding the same ring buffer so the sink never drains. ffmpeg-decoded items accept a short gap.
12. **Speed with pitch kept.** Video soundtracks use ffmpeg's `atempo` chain (each stage 0.5–2×, chained to reach 3×) inside the video player. Audio items use an in-process WSOLA time-stretcher (`fm-media::stretch`) after resampling, at the device rate, so speed needs no ffmpeg and works for every format the app decodes (revised 2026-11-12: routing audio through ffmpeg would have tied speed to an optional tool that most Linux and fresh Windows installs lack). At 1× no stretcher runs. A speed change restarts the audio segment at the current position, like a seek; within a segment the reported position is `start + played × speed`. Video follows the audio clock.
13. **Subtitles.** Every text format (SRT, WebVTT, ASS/SSA, embedded text tracks) becomes plain timed cues: SRT/WebVTT are parsed in `fm-media`, while ASS/SSA files and embedded tracks are first converted to SubRip by `ffmpeg -f srt` on a worker. The UI draws the cues as an overlay with one user-chosen style, so subtitles look the same whatever their source. This matters for low-vision users, and it is why ASS styling is dropped on purpose. Delay is just a clock offset. Bitmap tracks (PGS, VobSub) cannot be restyled; 6.2 shows them over the picture, honouring size/position where possible, and changing such a track restarts only the video decoder.
14. **Hardware decoding.** ffmpeg is started with the platform's `-hwaccel` (vaapi, d3d11va, videotoolbox) plus `hwdownload` to RGBA; failures or unsupported codecs restart the video decoder in software at the current position. A preference turns it off.
15. **Audiobooks and resume.** A `Book` groups files (a folder or an m4b) into one timeline with chapters from `ffprobe` (`-show_chapters`) or per file. Resume, bookmarks and finished state live in one owner-only store keyed by `VfsUri` plus size and mtime, capped at 500 items (100 bookmarks each). Only audiobooks and video are remembered; music never is.
16. **Sleep timer and A-B loop.** Both are session state ticked by the media engine: the sleep timer ends with a 10 s volume ramp; A-B loop is a seek when the clock passes B.
17. **System media controls.** `souvlaki` (MPRIS through its zbus feature to avoid libdbus, SMTC, macOS Now Playing) sits behind a small trait in `fm-app`; events dispatch only `media.*` commands. Windows SMTC and macOS need the native window handle, so `fm-app` may need `raw-window-handle` back (removed in 0.1.3 as unused).
18. **Exactly one Media pane.** `Layout` enforces it in `pane.kind.set`, Play and workspace restore (first wins, the rest become Files).
19. **ffmpeg discovery, not bundling.** Originally the release workflow was to bundle pinned LGPL sidecars. Superseded by `managed-ffmpeg`: installers ship no ffmpeg (bundling cost ~90 MB per installer and duplicated system copies); `find_tool` checks next to the executable, `PATH` and package-manager locations, then the app-managed folder that Windows fills from a pinned, SHA-256-verified download.
20. **Delivery in slices (one change).** A: `fm-media` pipeline, `symphonia`, Preview sound, `AudioOwner`. B: Media pane, queue, playlists, commands. C: A/V sync, tracks, fullscreen, remote spool, hand-off. D: gapless, EQ, speed, A-B loop, sleep timer. E: subtitles, hardware decoding. F: audiobooks, resume, bookmarks. G: media keys/MPRIS, ffmpeg bundling, docs, hardening.
21. **YouTube-style keyboard controls.** Use the exact bindings in the `media-pane` keyboard requirement, including Space/K, J/L, arrows, numeric seeking, F/Esc, Shift+N/P, paused frame stepping, speed and subtitle toggles. Route each event once through the input-ownership rules before dispatching engine commands; terminals, text input, modal dialogs and focused interactive widgets take precedence. Preview shares only the basic single-file transport bindings and dispatches its own commands, never queue commands. Show shortcut help and tooltips; do not extend Preview with Media-only features.
22. **Non-destructive queue actions.** Add appends, Play next inserts after the current item and schedules that batch before shuffle/history/repeat-one, and Play inserts there and starts immediately. No current item means insertion at the front. Existing queue rows play in place. Playback never automatically removes entries. Preview Play transfers position and stops its decoder; Add/Play next leave both players' current state unchanged.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **ALSA build dependency on Linux** → approved exception; CI installs the headers and the `.deb` declares `libasound2`.
- **A/V drift or stutter with two subprocesses** → audio master clock, frame dropping, and a drift test with a fake sink.
- **Seek latency from restarting decoders** → accept for v1; keyframe-aligned seeks for video.
- **ffmpeg not present on first run** → in-process audio formats still play; video shows an actionable hint, and Windows offers the managed download.
- **Scope** → this is one large change; slices A–G each leave the tree green and releasable, and any slice can be deferred without breaking the others.
- **Hardware decoding is flaky across drivers** → automatic software fallback, off switch, log entry on fallback.
- **Speed and gapless** → gapless hand-over still works at any speed: the stretcher is flushed at the item end and a fresh one starts for the next item, so a few milliseconds are cross-faded rather than joined sample-exactly; at 1× the join stays sample-exact.
- **Platform media-control crates** → best-effort registration, never blocks playback.
- **Hostile media and playlists** → decoders stay out of process with timeouts, playlist size/entry caps, path validation (see `security` delta).
- **Audio from a closed pane surprises users** → status-bar indicator with a stop button; pref to stop on close.
- **Memory** → only the newest video frames and a bounded PCM ring buffer are kept.
